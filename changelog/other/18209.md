- Deprecated `EHorizonGraphPath.edges`, `IncidentInfo.alertcCodes`, and `SubgraphEdge.innerEdgeIds`/`outerEdgeIds` in favor of new primitive-array-typed accessors (`edgesArray`, `alertcCodesArray`, `innerEdgeIdsArray`/`outerEdgeIdsArray`), which avoid per-element boxing. - Breaking (experimental only): `AdasRoadItem.lanes` and `AdasSpeedLimitRestriction.lanes` changed from `List<Byte>` to `ByteArray` to avoid per-element boxing. Both classes are `@ExperimentalPreviewMapboxNavigationAPI`.
