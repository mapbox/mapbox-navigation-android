- Added `MapboxEvViewOptions.Builder#withAlwaysShownStations`, taking a list of `AlwaysShownStation` (station `id` and `coordinate`). These stations are always shown on the map even when the active filters (operators, availability, charging power, payment methods, amenities, connector types) would otherwise hide them. A station is loaded by its ID only when its tile is in view and it is not already in the filtered result. A station whose ID is also in `withExcludeStationIds` stays excluded.
